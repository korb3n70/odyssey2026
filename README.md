Back to 1978: The Odyssey 2100 is reborn on the Commodore 64! 
Are you ready to take a leap back in time? Let me introduce you to my latest project: a complete and enhanced revival of the historic Magnavox/Philips Odyssey 2100 console from 1978, rebuilt from scratch for our beloved Commodore 64. This isn't just a simple nostalgic port, but a true "Director's Cut"!
Pure Code and Modular Architecture The project was developed entirely in pure Assembly (Bare Metal). Under the hood beats a rock-solid modular architecture: a central Hub (Menu Select) sorts and launches the 6 independent game modules (Tennis, Hockey, Football, Handball, Breakout, and Flipper). The code accesses the hardware directly (CIA and VIC-II chips), bypassing the KERNAL to ensure extreme performance, zero input lag, and flawless memory management.
Advanced Physics and Brand New Features We took the original '78 concepts and injected them with modern steroids:
•	Dynamic Physics: Implemented the "whip" effect (English/Spin Arcade) that allows you to cut and deflect the ball's trajectory based on the point of impact and the paddle's inertia.
•	Gravity and Collisions: Real inertia and gravity for Flipper, pixel-perfect collisions, and Vectrex-style explosions.
•	New Power-Ups: Moving bumpers, Multiball, giant paddles, and safety barriers.
•	Reactive AI programmed to intelligently challenge those playing in single-player mode.
100% Real Hardware The entire system has been rigorously tested on real hardware using the C64 Ultimate and played with Paddles. To ensure maximum fidelity and "crisp" controls, I wrote advanced key Debounce algorithms and Anti-Jitter filters for the paddle potentiometers (featuring dynamic dual-port reading). The result? Ultra-fluid movements, zero hardware jitter, and millimeter-perfect precision at 50Hz.
